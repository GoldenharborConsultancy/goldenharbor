How to Check Your CTOS Score in Malaysia

Updated: May 2026 · 5 min read

Your CTOS score may be one factor in a bank's credit assessment. Income, commitments, documents and the bank's own criteria also matter; a score alone does not determine approval or pricing.

This guide explains how to access CTOS reports, check which options include a score and review your credit information before applying.

What is a CTOS Score?

CTOS is a credit reporting agency in Malaysia. Its credit score ranges from 300 to 850 and reflects information about your credit history and repayment behaviour.

This is separate from CCRIS, Bank Negara Malaysia's credit information system. Check the contents of the report you obtain; banks decide which information to use in their assessment.

Method 1: CTOS Basic Report

The MyCTOS Basic Report does not include a CTOS score or CCRIS information. Check CTOS's current product details and access terms before choosing a report:

  1. Visit ctoscredit.com.my and review the available report options
  2. Register with your IC number, name, and email
  3. Verify your identity — you may need to upload a photo of your IC
  4. Access your chosen report — confirm what information it includes
💡 Tip: A basic credit report and a report containing a credit score are different products. Check current prices and contents directly with CTOS.

Method 2: CTOS Mobile Access

Download the CTOS Mobile app from Google Play or Apple App Store.

  1. Register and verify your IC number
  2. Review the report's current price and contents before paying
  3. Access the report after completing the provider's required steps

Report availability and contents depend on the product and verification process. The report does not reveal a bank's internal lending decision.

Reviewing Your Credit Information

These steps can help you understand and manage your credit commitments. They do not guarantee a score change or loan approval:

  • Review payment due dates — contact the lender if you are struggling to meet repayments
  • Check credit card balances — understand outstanding amounts and repayment obligations
  • Review recent applications — consider existing commitments before applying again
  • Check unfamiliar entries — keep supporting documents and query possible errors
  • Review account changes carefully — consider costs and commitments before opening or closing accounts

Common Questions

How often should We check my CTOS score?

Review your credit information before applying and when you need to check an unfamiliar entry or update. Allow time to query possible errors through the provider's process.

Does checking my own score affect it?

Checking your own CTOS report is a self-check, not a loan application. See CTOS's current guidance on score enquiries and check with a lender before authorising a credit application.

Can We check someone else's CTOS?

Use the provider's identity and consent procedures. Do not access another person's report without appropriate authorisation; joint applicants should follow the bank's instructions.
