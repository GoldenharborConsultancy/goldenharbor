Documents Needed for Home Loan in Malaysia

Updated: May 2026 · 5 min read

Applying for a home loan? Incomplete or incorrect documents can delay assessment. The bank may request additional information before making a decision.

Use this checklist as a preparation guide for salaried, self-employed and business-owner applicants. Items apply only where relevant; confirm the chosen bank's current requirements and document periods.

📋 Home Loan Document Preparation Checklist

A. Basic Personal Documents
  • MyKad (front & back) — yours and any joint borrowers Provide clear copies in the format requested by the bank
  • Birth certificate (if applying for young borrower / first home scheme) For certain schemes only
  • Marriage certificate (if requested for a joint application) Confirm certification requirements with the bank
B. Income Documents — Salaried Employees
  • Recent payslips Confirm the required period; include income and deduction details
  • Recent EPF statement (if applicable) Obtain through KWSP and confirm the required period
  • Recent bank statements Confirm the required period; show income and account activity
  • Employment confirmation letter Signed by employer, stamped if possible
  • EA Form or tax assessment Confirm the required periods with the bank
C. Income Documents — Self-Employed / Business Owners
  • Form B / Income Tax Return Confirm the required years; provide accurate declared income
  • Tax payment receipts and relevant tax documents As requested by the bank
  • Business and personal bank statements Cover the period requested by the bank
  • SSM registration certificate Valid & up to date
  • Business profile from SSM Shows business address, partners, nature of business
  • EPF statements (if applicable) Ask whether these are relevant to your application
  • Audited / management accounts (if requested) Confirm the periods and format with the bank
D. Property Documents
  • Sale & Purchase Agreement (SPA) Signed & stamped — the main document
  • Copy of property title (Geran / Master Title) From developer or current owner
  • Valuation report (if requested) Confirm valuation requirements with the bank
  • Booking form / letter of offer from developer For new projects
E. Additional Documents (If Applicable)
  • Letter of explanation (if you have past CCRIS issues) Be honest — explain what happened and what changed
  • Rental income documents (if using rental as additional income) Tenancy agreement + bank statements showing rent credited
  • Supporting documents for commission / bonus / overtime Confirm accepted evidence and the required period
  • Joint borrower's complete documents Same set of docs as main applicant
💡 Pro Tip: Ask the bank which digital copies and originals it accepts. Keep the relevant originals available for any verification requested.

Common Mistakes That Delay Your Home Loan

  • Blurry copies — provide legible, complete copies in the bank's requested format
  • Wrong statement period — confirm which months and accounts the bank requests
  • Missing pages — make sure every page of your bank statement is included
  • Old EPF statements — check the bank's recency requirements
  • Inconsistent signatures — your signature on the application form must match your IC

Which Bank to Choose?

Different banks have different policies for:

  • Income requirements — confirm accepted income types and any minimum threshold
  • Affordability assessment — ask how the bank assesses income and commitments
  • Interest rates — compare current rates, fees and repayment terms
  • Margin of financing — confirm the available amount and deposit requirements

Frequently Asked Questions

Can We apply for a home loan online?

Check the chosen bank's official application channels and accepted document formats. Online availability depends on the product and your circumstances.

How long does home loan approval take?

Assessment times vary by bank, product and application. Document checks or requests for additional information may affect timing; ask the bank for its current estimate.

Can We apply with multiple banks at once?

Consider existing and pending applications before applying again. Ask each bank about its process and credit checks; there is no universal number of applications that guarantees a particular outcome.
