Documents Needed for Personal & SME Loan in Malaysia

Updated: 15 October 2026 · 5 min read

Incomplete or incorrect documents can delay assessment of personal and SME loan applications. The bank may request additional information before making a decision.

Start with the common documents, then use the section for salaried, self-employed or SME applicants. Conditional items apply only to your circumstances or when requested. Confirm the chosen bank's current checklist, statement periods and accepted formats; the bank may request additional documents during assessment.

📋 Document Preparation Checklist

A. Common Starting Documents
  • MyKad or other identification accepted by the bank Provide clear copies; joint applicants and business representatives follow the bank's identity requirements
  • Application details and loan purpose Use the bank's current form and explain the amount and purpose; income evidence depends on the applicant type below
B. Income Documents — Salaried Employees (Personal Loan)
  • Recent payslips Confirm the required period; include income and deduction details
  • Recent EPF statement (if applicable) Obtain through KWSP and confirm the required period
  • Recent bank statements Confirm the required period; show income and account activity
  • Employment confirmation letter (if requested) Confirm what the employer needs to provide and whether certification is required
  • EA Form or tax documents (if requested) Confirm which documents and reporting periods the bank accepts
C. Income Documents — Self-Employed Applicants

Freelancers, e-hailing drivers and online sellers may need different income evidence. Our self-employed application guide explains how to organise the records; the bank decides which alternatives it accepts.

  • Relevant income tax returns and filing acknowledgements Confirm the correct return for your tax position and the periods requested; ask a tax adviser if unsure
  • Tax payment receipts (where applicable) Provide these if the bank requests evidence of tax paid
  • Business and personal bank statements Cover the period requested by the bank
  • SSM registration and business profile (where applicable) Confirm the records needed for your business type; keep registration details current
  • Invoices, contracts or platform payout reports Use the evidence relevant to your work and match received payments to bank entries
  • EPF statements (if applicable) Ask whether these are relevant to your application
  • Audited / management accounts (if requested) Confirm the periods and format with the bank
D. SME / Business Applicants

Use the checklist for the borrowing entity, not just the owner's personal income. The bank may request the following, depending on the entity, product and financing purpose:

  • Business loan proposal / purpose letter Explain what the loan is for (working capital, expansion, equipment, etc.)
  • Business registration, owner/director details and signing authority Provide the records and authorisations requested for the borrowing entity
  • Financial statements and relevant tax records Ask whether management or audited accounts are required and for which periods
  • List of trade debtors & creditors (if requested) Explain amounts owed to and by the business
  • Supplier / customer contracts (if relevant) Support the stated purpose and explain ongoing business activity
  • Business bank statements Confirm the required period; distinguish business and personal activity
  • Company stamp / chop (if requested) Follow the bank's current signing and form requirements
E. Conditional Supporting Documents
  • Birth certificate or marriage certificate (if requested) Relevant only to particular schemes or circumstances; confirm any certification needed
  • Letter of explanation (if you have past CCRIS issues) Be honest — explain what happened and what changed
  • Rental income documents (if using rental as additional income) Tenancy agreement + bank statements
  • Supporting documents for commission / bonus / overtime Confirm accepted evidence and the required period
  • Joint applicant's or guarantor's documents (if applicable) The bank specifies the evidence needed for each person's role
💡 Pro Tip: Ask the bank which digital copies and originals it accepts. Keep full, unaltered records, including all statement pages. A prepared checklist is a starting point, not a guarantee of approval or a limit on what else the bank may request.

Common Mistakes That Delay Your Loan

  • Blurry copies — provide legible, complete copies in the bank's requested format
  • Wrong statement period — confirm which months and accounts the bank requests
  • Missing pages — make sure every page of your bank statement is included
  • Old EPF statements — check the bank's recency requirements
  • Inconsistent signatures — Use a consistent signature and follow the bank's identity and signature-verification requirements.
  • SSM not updated — check registration details and the bank's current document requirements

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply for a personal loan online?

Check the chosen bank's official application channels and accepted document formats. Online availability depends on the product and your circumstances.

How long does loan approval take?

Assessment times vary by bank, product and application. Ask the bank for its current estimate and whether further documents are needed.

Can I apply with multiple banks at once?

Consider existing and pending applications before applying again. Ask each bank about its process and credit checks; we cannot predict which bank will approve an application.
